PlaneChat · Privacy Policy

Your messages never touch a server.

Effective 16 October 2026. PlaneChat is made by Black Bao Studio (Noël Ruault Martínez).

  • No account, no phone number, no email.
  • No servers, no internet requests, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party code.
  • We, the developer, collect nothing and can see nothing, unless you email us a report.

How PlaneChat works

PlaneChat sends messages directly between nearby phones over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. When the person you're writing to is out of range, other phones running PlaneChat pass the message along. Nothing goes through the internet or through any computer we run.

What stays on your phone

  • Your identity keys, created on your phone the first time you open the app, stored in the iOS Keychain with "this device only" protection, so they are never synced to iCloud or restored to another device.
  • Your settings: the name you chose, who can see you, whether your phone relays, which radios are on, your friends and blocked lists, the people you accepted in the public room, the private rooms you joined, and which version of the Terms you agreed to.
  • Your conversations are saved on this phone, encrypted with a key kept in the Keychain on this device only, and left out of backups. The newest 1,024 messages and their edits are kept. Delete them by swiping a chat in Chats, by deleting single messages, from Settings → Delete all chats, or by deleting the app. Deletion is not a forensic wipe.
  • A stranger's public-room messages that you haven't accepted are held in memory only, never saved, and are gone when the app closes.

Deleting the app deletes your settings and conversations. iOS may keep the Keychain identity until you reset the phone; it is never sent anywhere.

What nearby phones receive

  • Your name and public key. With visibility set to "Everyone", phones nearby that run PlaneChat see the name you chose and a public key, so they can show your bubble. With "Friends only", this is sent sealed to your friends and nobody else can see you.
  • Your messages, end-to-end encrypted. Private chats can only be read by you and the other person. Phones that relay them can't read them or see who they're for. Private-room messages can only be read by people who know the room's code.
  • Relayed messages are held briefly. To reach someone who walks back into range, a relaying phone keeps sealed messages it can't read in memory for up to 20 minutes, then drops them. You can turn relaying off in Settings.
  • The public room ("Everyone in range") can be received by any phone nearby running PlaneChat. Each person only sees your messages after accepting you, but anyone can accept. Don't share anything there you wouldn't say out loud.

Reports

If you report someone, PlaneChat first shows what the report contains: the reported person's name, their code and public key and, when you report a message, its original text, time and message ID, plus anything you add. It then opens an email to us, or lets you copy or share the report if your phone has no Mail account. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. We use reports only to deal with abuse and delete them once handled.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth: to find and talk to nearby phones.
  • Local Network: to talk to nearby phones over peer-to-peer Wi-Fi.
  • Notifications, optional: to tell you when someone asks to chat or messages you while PlaneChat is in the background. Message previews can be turned off in Settings.

PlaneChat doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Age

PlaneChat is for people 18 and older and collects no personal data from anyone.
