PlaneChat · Support

Chat with people nearby, no internet needed.

PlaneChat connects iPhones directly over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi, and passes messages phone to phone to reach people further away.

Something not working, or an idea? Email contact@blackbao.studio with your iPhone model and iOS version.

Getting connected

Nobody shows up on the Nearby screen
  1. Make sure the other person has PlaneChat open too.
  2. In airplane mode, switch Wi-Fi and Bluetooth back on from Control Center. You don't need to join a network.
  3. Check Settings → PlaneChat and allow Bluetooth and Local Network.
  4. In PlaneChat's Settings, check that at least one radio is on, and that "Who can see me" is set the way you want.
How far does it reach?

Directly, about the length of a room or a few rows of seats. Beyond that, other phones running PlaneChat pass your message along, so a busy space can carry it much further. Every phone with relaying on helps.

Does it work in the background?

Bluetooth keeps working in a limited way while the app is in the background. Peer-to-peer Wi-Fi only works while PlaneChat is open, so keep it on screen when you're waiting for a reply.

Chatting

Why do I have to ask before chatting?

Nobody can message you until you accept their "Wanna chat?" request, so strangers can't spam you. Friends can message you straight away.

What do the ticks mean?

A clock means the message is on its way; two ticks mean the other phone received and decrypted it. Messages can take a moment when they travel through other phones.

Can I edit a message?

Yes. Touch and hold one of your messages and choose Edit. Everyone in the chat sees it marked "Edited", and can see every earlier version under Edit history.

Rooms and friends

"Everyone on board" is a public room for everyone nearby. Private rooms are created by typing the same code on each phone; only people with the code can read them. Touch and hold someone's bubble to add them as a friend.

Someone is bothering me

Touch and hold their bubble or one of their messages and choose Block: you stop seeing them and they can't ask to chat or message you. Choose Report… to email us what happened; it blocks them too. Set "Who can see me" to Friends only to hide from everyone else. See the terms of use.

How do I know I'm talking to the right person?

Each chat shows a short code. Compare it in person, or ask about something only the two of you know.

Where are my messages saved?

They aren't. Conversations live in memory and disappear when you close the app. See the privacy policy for the details.
