What you can do about abuse

  • Accept first: a stranger's messages only reach your screen after you accept them, in a private chat or in the public room.
  • Delete: touch and hold any message and choose Delete. It's removed from your phone at once.
  • Block: touch and hold someone's bubble or message and choose Block. You stop seeing them, and they can't ask to chat or message you.
  • Report: choose Report… on a bubble, message or chat. PlaneChat shows what the report contains, blocks them straight away, and helps you email it to us.
  • Hide offensive words: on by default in Settings, it masks slurs and strong profanity in what other people send. PlaneChat also asks before you send words on that list.
  • Friends only: in Settings, makes you invisible to everyone except your friends.

How we act on reports

Reports go to contact@blackbao.studio and get a reply within 24 hours. PlaneChat has no servers and no accounts: messages live only on the phones that sent and received them, so we cannot delete them from other phones or remove anyone from the app. By the time you write to us, your phone has already removed their messages and blocked them.

You're responsible for what you send. Messages in the public room, Everyone in range, can be received by any phone nearby running PlaneChat. Private chats and private rooms are end-to-end encrypted, but the people you send them to can still copy or show them to others.
