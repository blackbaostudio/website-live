Be decent to the people around you.

Effective 10 October 2026. By using PlaneChat you agree to these terms.

Zero tolerance. PlaneChat has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Don't send harassment, threats, hate speech, sexually explicit material, content involving minors, spam, scams, or anything illegal where you are.

What you can do about abuse

  • Block: touch and hold someone's bubble or message and choose Block. You stop seeing them, and they can't ask to chat or message you.
  • Report: choose Report… on a bubble, message or chat. It opens an email to us with what was said and the sender's code, and blocks them straight away.
  • Hide offensive words: on by default in Settings, it masks slurs and strong profanity in what other people send.
  • Friends only: in Settings, makes you invisible to everyone except your friends.

How we act on reports

PlaneChat has no servers and no accounts, so messages never pass through us and we can't delete them from anyone's phone. We review every report within 24 hours and, where something illegal is involved, cooperate with the authorities using the details in the report.

Your responsibility

You're responsible for what you send. Messages in the public room can be read by anyone nearby running PlaneChat. Private chats and private rooms are end-to-end encrypted, but the people you send them to can still copy or show them to others.

No warranty

PlaneChat is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. Delivery depends on radios and on other phones nearby, so messages can be delayed or lost. Don't rely on it for emergencies.
