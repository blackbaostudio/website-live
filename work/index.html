Black Bao Studio — work

Black Bao Studio, a small studio in Alicante crafting meticulously engineered websites. SRE-grade reliability, on-point UI/UX, zero framework bloat. We aim to make every byte you download worth it.

Featured work

  • DEEP Estate Agency — An uplift for a private real-estate agency on the Costa Blanca: the new sections from their UX team, silent video previews that load only when seen, and their Buying in Spain dossier rebuilt as live web pages instead of a PDF.
  • Gatehouse — Cloud-controlled gate openers. A relay box on the gate holds one outbound connection to a Cloudflare Durable Object, and the owner opens the gate from a phone, from anywhere, through an installable web app with magic-link sign-in and a mandatory passkey. Every command is signed by the server and checked by the firmware, so no single leaked secret opens a gate on its own.
  • Educación a Bordo — An exam-prep platform for Spanish recreational boat licences. Students pick temas, sit a timed simulacro or study untimed, and are scored exactly the way the real exam marks, then follow their streak, progress and history. One codebase white-labels to a new academy from a single config file.
